Voice AI for customer service

The Domus call center

Getting a no-show boarding fee back means calling Azul's call center, getting through the IVR and waiting on hold. On one Sunday I measured, the wait came close to an hour. The Domus call center walks that path and lets the team follow every call live.

  1. 01

    Each call starts from a ready request

    The holder's and ticket's details come from the no-show queue before dialing, so the conversation never depends on memory.

  2. 02

    The IVR is navigated automatically

    The system presses the keys on Azul's IVR and recognizes when a human agent comes on the line.

    Measured on real calls
  3. 03

    Clara handles the request

    She follows a step-by-step script to the protocol number: booking code, cancellation, fee amount and refund method.

    Tested with a simulated agent
  4. 04

    The team follows along live

    Both sides are recorded separately, the transcript appears seconds after each line with ID and card numbers masked, and whoever is monitoring gets an alert when the agent's voice stops coming through.

About

I came from operations. That's how I know what's worth automating.

I'm from Rio de Janeiro. I spent about two years at a high-volume miles desk, issuing tickets and training colleagues on my way to a senior role. That's where I learned where time gets lost: on the phone, in statements that don't reconcile, in the same screen opened a hundred times.

I started building for my own job and cut the time per ticket in half. That led to Labs Aero and then to the Domus ecosystem, including the voice call center.

I work like a one-person company with AI as my team: Claude Code with MCPs (Playwright, Chrome, Supabase) to build, test and ship, with automated tests before each release.
